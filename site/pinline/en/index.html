PinLine

Keep your place while reading

PinLine is a line on your screen that you place yourself. It stays with the line you are reading, even when you scroll or look away for a moment.

Why PinLine?

Look away for a moment, at your phone, another window or your second screen? Then you have to search for where you were. PinLine solves that: a line you place under your line of text yourself, which glides along with the text. When you look back, you see straight away where you left off.

What PinLine does

Pinned to the page or free

The line glides along with the text, or stays in the same place on your screen.

One screen or more

Works on a single screen and on multiple screens, even with different scaling.

Read along

The line stays put while the text slides underneath.

Back to your line

Scrolled your line out of view? PinLine takes you back to it.

Bookmark with your own photo

Turn the line into a strip with your own photo.

Everything with the mouse

No shortcuts to remember.

Your screen stays yours

PinLine only looks at a narrow strip around the line to find your line of text again. Those images stay in memory and are never saved or sent. No account, no ads, no tracking. Privacy →

Requirements

Windows 11, or Windows 10 version 2004 or later. Available in Dutch, English, German, French, Spanish and Italian.
