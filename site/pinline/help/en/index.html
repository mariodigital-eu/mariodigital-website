PinLine

Keep your place while reading

PinLine is a line on your screen that you place yourself. It stays with the line you are reading, even when you scroll or glance at your other screen.

Getting started

  1. Click the PinLine button on the taskbar, or the PinLine icon at the bottom right. Can't see the icon? Look under the ^ arrow.
  2. The pointer turns into a crosshair. Click just below the line you are reading. Right-click or Esc cancels.
  3. The line is pinned to the page right away: when you scroll, it glides along with the text.
  4. Glancing at your other screen? When you look back, you see straight away where you were.

Line

Pinned or free
Click the dot with the padlock, or double-click the line. Pinned: the line glides along with the text. Free: the line stays in the same place on the screen.
Moving
Grab the line (up to about 1 mm above or below it) and drag. Outside that zone you can select text as usual.
Shortening
Drag the dot to the right to shorten from the left, or drag the handle on the right to change the length. New lines get the same length.
Reading along
Hold down the line or the dot and scroll with the mouse wheel: the line stays put and the text slides underneath. When you let go, the line pins itself to the line of text below it.
Line out of view
If you scroll the line out of view, ▲ or ▼ appears. Scroll back, or click ▲ or ▼: PinLine then scrolls back to your line by itself.
Scrolling on the line
The mouse wheel works as usual, even when the pointer is on the line.

Bookmark

Turning it on
Right-click the icon → Style → Bookmark. The first time, the design window opens.
Photo
Choose a photo. Photos from your phone are turned upright automatically. PinLine keeps a reduced copy.
Placing it
Drag in the preview to move the photo. Zoom in or out with the slider or the mouse wheel; when zoomed out, the rest is filled with the line color.
Finishing
Choose the height, a border, your own text and the opacity. The preview shows exactly how it will look on your screen, including the dot and the handle.
Using it
Everything works as with the line: pinned or free, dragging, shortening and reading along. Back to the line via Style → Line.

Menu

Opening
Right-click the icon, the line or the dot.
Place line
Place a new line. Left-clicking the icon does the same.
Pinned to page
Switch between pinned and free.
Hide line
Temporarily hide the line; show it again from the same menu.
Style and design
Line or bookmark, and designing the bookmark.
Color, thickness, glide
How the line looks, and how calmly it glides along. Use the slider to choose anything from fast to very soft.
Pin new line right away
Whether a new line sticks to the text straight away.
Show line in screenshot
Normally the line does not appear in screenshots. Choose this and take your screenshot within half a minute, for example with Alt+Print Screen.
Button on the taskbar
PinLine as a button next to your other programs. Click it to place a line. Right-click and choose Pin to taskbar to keep it.
Always show icon
Opens the Windows taskbar settings, where you make the PinLine icon always visible.
Language
The language of PinLine, or the same as Windows.

Tips & privacy

Quick access
Pin the taskbar button. Right-click that button for Place line, Toggle pinned/free and the Manual.
First scroll
In a new program, PinLine first learns how far the text moves per wheel click. After that, the line glides along straight away.
Multiple screens
PinLine works on every screen, even when the screens use different scaling.
Privacy
PinLine looks at a narrow strip of the screen to find the line of text again. Those images stay in memory only: nothing is saved or sent.
