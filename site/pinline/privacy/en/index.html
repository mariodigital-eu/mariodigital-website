PinLine privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

In short

PinLine works entirely on your own computer. You don't need an account, there are no ads, and PinLine doesn't collect statistics. PinLine doesn't send anything about you or your screen.

What PinLine looks at on your screen

To find your line of text again when you scroll, PinLine looks at a narrow strip of the screen around the line. Those images are processed in memory and discarded straight away. They are not saved and not sent.

By default, the line itself does not appear in screenshots. Only when you choose ‘Show line in screenshot’ in the menu does it appear for half a minute.

Mouse and keyboard

PinLine responds to the mouse wheel, to scroll keys such as the arrow keys and Page Down, and to Esc. PinLine doesn't use any other keys, and what you type is never stored or sent.

What PinLine stores on your computer

PinLine keeps a few files in the folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\MarioDigital\PinLine on your own computer:

  • your settings, such as colour, length, language and the bookmark design;
  • for each program, how far the text moves per wheel click, with only the name of that program;
  • a reduced copy of the photo you choose for the bookmark;
  • a log file with technical details, such as times, program names and screen positions. This helps with solving problems.

These files never leave your computer. You can remove them at any time by deleting that folder.

Internet

PinLine doesn't connect to the internet by itself. If you click the MarioDigital logo or ‘Privacy policy’, your own browser opens the website mariodigital.eu.

Microsoft Store

If you install PinLine from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the installation and updates. Microsoft's privacy statement applies to that: privacy.microsoft.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top.
