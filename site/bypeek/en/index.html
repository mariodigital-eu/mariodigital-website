Why ByPeek?

Opening the Bybit app, logging in and looking for your card balance takes time, and at the checkout you don't have it. ByPeek puts your card's spendable balance right on your home screen. After a few seconds it hides the amount again by itself.

What ByPeek does

Home screen widget

Small, medium, large or playful. Click to see your balance; it hides itself after 10 seconds.

Payment notifications

Optional notification after a payment or deposit, without ByPeek seeing who it was with.

Low balance warning

The amount and widget border turn orange when you drop below your own limit.

Family can watch along

Share the balance for free with family members via a QR code.

Bybit and Bybit EU

Works with both, and with the coins your card uses (EUR, USDC, USDT, BTC and more).

Try the demo first

See how it works without a Bybit account.

Safe and private

ByPeek only uses a read-only API key that you create yourself at Bybit. With it, ByPeek can never trade or withdraw money. Your key stays on your phone. No account, no server, no ads, no tracking. Privacy →
